ABOUT

Bless the underground.

The Haze is an alternative rock band formed in Cantabria in 2023. Influenced by 90s rock and grunge, the band looks to create its own identity through original songs, raw energy and an honest approach to music.

From the rehearsal room to the stage, intensity and connection are at the core of The Haze. What started as a shared need to make music from within became a project built by friends who make music together.
